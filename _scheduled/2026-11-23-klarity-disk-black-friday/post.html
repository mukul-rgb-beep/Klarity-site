Klarity Disk Black Friday 2026: $2.99 Mac Disk Analyzer

$2.99 in most countries from 27 November to 1 December 2026, usually $6.99. No code to enter, one payment, no subscription.

From 27 November to 1 December 2026, Klarity Disk is $2.99 on the Mac App Store in most countries. It is usually $6.99. There is no code to enter: the store page shows the lower price by itself. Until 27 November it still shows $6.99.

Klarity Disk is a Mac disk space analyzer that I build on my own. When your Mac says the disk is almost full and Storage settings shows you a long grey bar, Klarity shows you which folders hold the space.

What it does, in three pictures

1. A map of your disk, coloured by age

Scan your home folder, the whole disk or an external drive. Every folder becomes a rectangle as big as the space it takes, coloured by how long ago anything in it last changed. Big and red means large and untouched for three years or more, which is usually the first place to look. Hidden folders such as Library are part of the scan.

2. The large files, largest first

Open a folder such as Movies or Downloads, click Large files, and Klarity lists the files inside it, largest first, each with how long ago it changed. A forgotten 5 GB export from 2023 stands out. You can narrow the list to one kind of file, such as video.

3. Move to the Trash, carefully

You can move things to the Trash from the app. It always asks first. A folder over 1 GB needs a slide to confirm, risky places get a second warning, and system folders are refused. Nothing is deleted outright: it goes to the Trash, and Klarity never empties the Trash for you.

Who it is not for

Klarity is not a cleaner. It does not hunt for duplicates, it deletes nothing on its own, and it cannot scan cloud drives. There is no free trial. If you want a free treemap, GrandPerspective is free from its own site; if a list sorted by size is all you need, OmniDiskSweeper is free too. Both are on my honest list of DaisyDisk alternatives, with what each does better than Klarity.
